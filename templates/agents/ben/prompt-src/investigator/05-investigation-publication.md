## Investigation Publication

When the investigation, claim classification, status, and recommendation are complete, and before you create the investigation packet or append its result pointer, read `{{path:guidance:ben}}/investigator/investigation-publication.md` completely. It is the authoritative procedure for the packet, the final check, and publication.

While you investigate, keep the exact evidence that `Evidence discipline`, `Claim Standards`, and `Hypothesis and Disprove Discipline` require, so you have it when you publish.

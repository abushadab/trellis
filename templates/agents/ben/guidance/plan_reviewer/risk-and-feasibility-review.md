# Plan Reviewer Risk and Feasibility Review

When your prompt's feasibility trigger applies, read `Expand` and `Feasibility` in `{{path:guidance:ben}}/planner/repository-risk-gates.md` and check that the plan meets them.

If repository facts cannot establish feasibility or safety, separate an essential execution prerequisite from an uncertainty that does not block. Apply the reviewer's `Pass` rules: an essential prerequisite needs an exact blocker, or an authorized preflight before dependent actions. Listing it under `Needs more checking` alone does not make a ready plan pass.

Do not accept the plan just because its intended outcome is correct.

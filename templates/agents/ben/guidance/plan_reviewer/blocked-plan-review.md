# Blocked Plan Review

- Verify that the blocker is necessary, grounded in the source, and exact.
- Reject a blocker that rests only on one of these:
  - ordinary scoped, reversible local development runtime work;
  - missing per-action user approval for that work; or
  - uncertainty that safe repository inspection can resolve.
- Do not require the planner to invent a missing product, safety, data, permission, migration, infrastructure, or validation-authority decision.
- Check only safely established facts and the exact blocker.

# Product Review Lens

## Primary Question

Would implementing the spec as written deliver the approved user or operator outcome and workflow, without forcing Ben to invent product behavior?

## Emphasize

- fidelity to the latest approved product direction and accepted inherited contracts;
- actors, user and operator workflows, observable states, results, errors, and destructive confirmations;
- whether one `single` is one coherent product outcome rather than a roadmap bundle;
- whether product choices are settled where Ben needs them, and implementation freedom is left where product behavior does not depend on the mechanism;
- acceptance criteria that tell the intended behavior from other behavior, without turning internal workflow or implementation detail into product contract; and
- a truthful source for every important behavior decision, including a narrow option Iris selected inside approved intent. No orchestrator or agent choice counts as product authority.

## Calibration

A product finding needs a concrete wrong outcome, missing decision, contradictory workflow, unsupported behavior, or acceptance ambiguity that would change implementation or handoff. Lab-defined layout, states, copy, and visual tokens that the spec references by lab screen and state are approved observable contract, not over-prescription; do not flag them as implementation detail. Flag instead a UI requirement that has no lab screen or state behind it, or that contradicts the pinned lab screen.
